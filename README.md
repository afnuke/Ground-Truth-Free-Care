# Ground Truth: Free Care

**See the free care a hospital actually gives back.**

An app in the Ground Truth series. Nonprofit hospitals pay no taxes on the understanding that they give care back to their community — and they report what they give in their own IRS Form 990 and Schedule H filings. Free Care reads those filings and shows what the hospitals themselves report: the charity care given, the executive pay, and the margin.

## The data rules

- Every figure comes from the hospital's own IRS Form 990 and Schedule H — the only source. No third-party estimates, no summaries of summaries.
- It starts locally, with **Prisma Health** and **Bon Secours St. Francis** in Greenville, SC.
- The app presents the record and never renders a verdict — what the numbers mean is for the reader to judge.

## How it works

Pick a hospital and see what its own filing says it gave back, set beside what it keeps and what its executives are paid. No ads, no accounts, no selling your data.
